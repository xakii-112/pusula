# Impact calibration on tick data

Generated by `tools/impact_report.py`. Y is the coefficient of `sigma_daily x sign(Q) x sqrt(|Q| / ADV)` for the return of a five-minute bucket on its signed flow. It measures aggregate order flow, not one desk's order, and tick-rule signs are noisy, which biases Y towards zero: read it as an order of magnitude. Registered cost models do not change because of this; a different Y is a new family's configuration.

## Stocks (Alpaca consolidated trades, tick rule)

40 current index members drawn at random, 6 sessions, two 60-minute windows each (5,545,948 trades, at most 4 pages per window, so the most liquid names are cut short). Pooled Y = **0.91** over 5544 five-minute buckets; median across windows 0.96; the registered cost model uses 0.7 and the gate doubles it to 1.4.
